## English

Message:

    BibTeX syntax error; this entry is skipped: {detail}

**What it checks.** An entry the BibTeX parser cannot read: unbalanced braces, a missing comma or quote. It is skipped, and BibTeX may skip it too or swallow the next entry.

**When it can be wrong.** The parser follows BibTeX's grammar; a construct only biber accepts may be reported.

**What to do.** Fix the syntax at the line given. If biber reads the entry and you build with biber, please report it.

To turn it off for the whole project: `ignore-rules = ["CIT007"]` in the project's settings.

## 中文

消息：

    BibTeX 语法错误，该条目会被跳过：{detail}

**检查什么。** BibTeX 解析器无法读取的条目：花括号不配对、缺少逗号或引号。该条目会被跳过，BibTeX 也可能跳过它，或把下一个条目一并吞掉。

**什么时候可能误报。** 解析器遵循 BibTeX 的语法；只有 biber 接受的写法可能被报告。

**怎么处理。** 在给出的行号处修正语法。如果 biber 能读取该条目且你用 biber 编译，请提交 issue。

对整个项目关闭此规则：在项目设置中写 `ignore-rules = ["CIT007"]`。
