## English

Message:

    {count} reference(s) were checked through {substitutes} because {sources} did not answer. What only {sources} knows (withdrawn papers, titles of earlier versions) was not checked; re-run later for a full check.

**What it checks.** arXiv did not answer and DataCite, which registers arXiv's DOIs, answered in its place. What only arXiv knows (withdrawn preprints, the titles of earlier versions) was not checked.

**When it can be wrong.** No case is known. If you meet one, please report it: false positives are the bugs we most want to hear about.

**What to do.** Re-run later for a full check.

To turn it off for the whole project: `ignore-rules = ["RUN002"]` in the project's settings.

## 中文

消息：

    有 {count} 条文献因 {sources} 未应答，改用 {substitutes} 核查。只有 {sources} 掌握的信息（论文是否撤回、早期版本的标题）这次未能核查；稍后重新运行可完整核查。

**检查什么。** arXiv 没有应答，由为 arXiv 登记 DOI 的 DataCite 代为应答。只有 arXiv 掌握的信息（预印本是否撤回、早期版本的标题）这次没有被核查。

**什么时候可能误报。** 目前没有已知的情形。如果遇到，请提交 issue：误报是我们最想知道的问题。

**怎么处理。** 稍后重新运行，即可完整核查。

对整个项目关闭此规则：在项目设置中写 `ignore-rules = ["RUN002"]`。
