# REF013 · year-differs

**Year differs from the record** · 年份与记录不符

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity · 严重度 | warning · 警告 |
| Fix · 修复 | `bib fix --level unsafe`: review the diff before applying it · `bib fix --level unsafe`：应用前请先检查差异 |

## English

Message:

    '{key}' gives the year {year}, but {source} records {found_years}.

**What it checks.** The year is none of the record's dates: print, online and issue dates, and for a preprint the years of its versions.

**When it can be wrong.** A conference paper dated by the conference rather than the proceedings, or a book's later printing. Publishers' online-first dates and digitised backfiles are handled where known.

**What to do.** Use the year of the version you cite. `bib fix --level unsafe` writes the record's year.

To silence it for one entry once you have checked it, put a comment above the entry:

    % preflight: ignore[REF013] reason="..."

To turn it off for the whole project: `ignore-rules = ["REF013"]` in the project's settings.

## 中文

消息：

    条目 '{key}' 的年份是 {year}，但 {source} 记录为 {found_years}。

**检查什么。** 条目的年份不在记录的任何日期之中：印刷、在线和期号日期，预印本还包括其各版本的年份。

**什么时候可能误报。** 会议论文按会议年份而非论文集出版年份标注，或图书的后续印次。出版社的在线优先日期和后来数字化的旧书记录，在已知的情况下已做处理。

**怎么处理。** 使用你所引版本的年份。`bib fix --level unsafe` 会写入记录中的年份。

核对之后，如需对某个条目消除此规则，在条目上方加注释：

    % preflight: ignore[REF013] reason="..."

对整个项目关闭此规则：在项目设置中写 `ignore-rules = ["REF013"]`。
