**Two entries look like the same work** · 两个条目疑似同一篇文献

| | |
|---|---|

| Fix · 修复 | by hand, as the message suggests · 按消息中的建议手动修改 |

## English

Message:

    Entries '{key}' and '{other}' look like the same work (same {reason}).

**What it checks.** Two entries with different keys that share a DOI, an arXiv ID, or their title and year (titles of four words or more). The paper then cites one work twice, under two numbers.

**When it can be wrong.** Two chapters of one book that both carry the book's DOI are told apart by their titles and not reported. A paper and its erratum, or a two-part work published in one year under one title, are real pairs.

**What to do.** Keep one entry and point the other's citations to it. If the two really are different works, silence it above either entry: `% preflight: ignore[CIT004]`.

To silence it for one entry once you have checked it, put a comment above the entry:

    % preflight: ignore[CIT004] reason="..."

To turn it off for the whole project: `ignore-rules = ["CIT004"]` in the project's settings.

## 中文

消息：

    条目 '{key}' 与 '{other}' 疑似同一篇文献（{reason}相同）。

**检查什么。** 两个键不同的条目有相同的 DOI、arXiv 编号，或相同的标题和年份（标题至少四个词）。这样论文会以两个编号重复引用同一篇作品。

**什么时候可能误报。** 同一本书的两个章节都带着书的 DOI 时，会按章节标题区分开，不会报告。论文与它的勘误、同一年以同一标题发表的上下两部分，是确实存在的成对条目。

**怎么处理。** 保留一个条目，把另一个的引用改为指向它。如果两者确实是不同的作品，在任一条目上方加注释消除：`% preflight: ignore[CIT004]`。

核对之后，如需对某个条目消除此规则，在条目上方加注释：

    % preflight: ignore[CIT004] reason="..."

对整个项目关闭此规则：在项目设置中写 `ignore-rules = ["CIT004"]`。
