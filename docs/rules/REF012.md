**Title differs from the record** · 标题与记录不符

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity · 严重度 | warning · 警告 |
| Fix · 修复 | `bib fix --level unsafe`: review the diff before applying it · `bib fix --level unsafe`：应用前请先检查差异 |

## English

Message:

    The title of '{key}' differs from {source}: "{found_title}" ({difference}).

**What it checks.** The title differs from the record's beyond capitals, punctuation and LaTeX markup: either it is far from it, or a few words are reworded (named in the message). Rewording is reported only when every title the work has had is known.

**When it can be wrong.** A title changed between preprint and publication (all known versions are compared), or a translated title of a work in another language (then an info).

**What to do.** Use the title of the version you cite. `bib fix --level unsafe` writes the record's title with its capitals protected.

To silence it for one entry once you have checked it, put a comment above the entry:

    % preflight: ignore[REF012] reason="..."

To turn it off for the whole project: `ignore-rules = ["REF012"]` in the project's settings.

## 中文

消息：

    条目 '{key}' 的标题与 {source} 的记录不符："{found_title}"（{difference}）。

**检查什么。** 标题与记录不符，且差异不只是大小写、标点和 LaTeX 标记：要么相差很大，要么有几个词被替换（消息中会列出）。只有在作品历来所有版本的标题都已知时，才会报告换词。

**什么时候可能误报。** 预印本与正式版本之间标题有改动（会与所有已知版本比较），或以译名引用其他语言的作品（此时只作为提示）。

**怎么处理。** 使用你所引版本的标题。`bib fix --level unsafe` 会写入记录中的标题，并保护其中的大写字母。

核对之后，如需对某个条目消除此规则，在条目上方加注释：

    % preflight: ignore[REF012] reason="..."

对整个项目关闭此规则：在项目设置中写 `ignore-rules = ["REF012"]`。
