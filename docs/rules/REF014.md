**Venue differs from the record** · 发表场所与记录不符

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity · 严重度 | warning · 警告 |
| Fix · 修复 | `bib fix --level unsafe`: review the diff before applying it · `bib fix --level unsafe`：应用前请先检查差异 |

## English

Message:

    '{key}' names {venue} as the venue, but {source} records {found_venue}.

**What it checks.** The journal or booktitle names another venue than the record's, after known acronyms and abbreviations are expanded.

**When it can be wrong.** A workshop co-located with a conference, a renamed journal, or proceedings published in a series (LNCS) under another name.

**What to do.** Use the venue of the version you cite. `bib fix --level unsafe` writes the record's venue.

To silence it for one entry once you have checked it, put a comment above the entry:

    % preflight: ignore[REF014] reason="..."

To turn it off for the whole project: `ignore-rules = ["REF014"]` in the project's settings.

## 中文

消息：

    条目 '{key}' 写的发表场所是 {venue}，但 {source} 记录为 {found_venue}。

**检查什么。** 期刊或会议名与记录中的发表场所不同（已展开已知的缩写和简称后再比较）。

**什么时候可能误报。** 与主会同期举办的研讨会、改过名的期刊，或以丛书名义（如 LNCS）出版的会议论文集。

**怎么处理。** 使用你所引版本的发表场所。`bib fix --level unsafe` 会写入记录中的发表场所。

核对之后，如需对某个条目消除此规则，在条目上方加注释：

    % preflight: ignore[REF014] reason="..."

对整个项目关闭此规则：在项目设置中写 `ignore-rules = ["REF014"]`。
