**Entry is never cited** · 条目未被引用

## English

Message:

    Entry '{key}' is never cited.

**What it checks.** An entry no citation uses. Reported when a manuscript is checked, not a bibliography on its own, and not when `\nocite{*}` includes every entry.

**When it can be wrong.** The entry is cited through a macro paper-preflight does not know (add it with `--cite-command`), or the `.bib` is shared by several papers.

**What to do.** Nothing is wrong with the paper. Delete the entry if it is a leftover; for a shared bibliography, turn the rule off in the project's settings: `ignore-rules = ["CIT003"]`.

To silence it for one entry once you have checked it, put a comment above the entry:

    % preflight: ignore[CIT003] reason="..."

To turn it off for the whole project: `ignore-rules = ["CIT003"]` in the project's settings.

## 中文

消息：

    条目 '{key}' 从未被引用。

**检查什么。** 没有被任何引用用到的条目。只在检查稿件时报告（单独检查参考文献文件时不报）；使用 `\nocite{*}` 收录全部条目时也不报。

**什么时候可能误报。** 条目是通过 paper-preflight 不认识的宏引用的（用 `--cite-command` 加上），或者这个 `.bib` 由多篇论文共用。

**怎么处理。** 论文本身没有问题。如果是遗留条目就删掉；共用的参考文献可以在项目设置中关闭此规则：`ignore-rules = ["CIT003"]`。

核对之后，如需对某个条目消除此规则，在条目上方加注释：

    % preflight: ignore[CIT003] reason="..."

对整个项目关闭此规则：在项目设置中写 `ignore-rules = ["CIT003"]`。
