**Reference not found in any source** · 所有来源均未找到该文献

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity · 严重度 | error · 错误 |
| Fix · 修复 | none · 无 |

## English

Message:

    '{key}' was not found in {sources}, and every source responded. Check that the work exists and that its title is correct.

**What it checks.** The work was searched for by its title in every source that searches titles, every source answered, and nothing matched. Not reported for references the indexes are known to miss (grey literature, very new or pre-1990 works, workshop papers, non-Latin titles): those are REF090.

**When it can be wrong.** A real work can be missing from every index: an internal report, a thesis, a regional journal, a work cited by a translated title. A badly garbled title does not match either.

**What to do.** Check that the work exists and that its title is written as published. If it exists and is just not indexed, silence it with the reason: `% preflight: ignore[REF003] reason="internal report"`.

To silence it for one entry once you have checked it, put a comment above the entry:

    % preflight: ignore[REF003] reason="..."

To turn it off for the whole project: `ignore-rules = ["REF003"]` in the project's settings.

## 中文

消息：

    在 {sources} 中均未找到 '{key}'，且所有来源都已正常应答。请确认该作品确实存在、标题无误。

**检查什么。** 按标题在所有支持标题检索的来源中检索了该作品，所有来源都正常应答，但没有任何匹配。对已知索引常常漏收的文献（灰色文献、很新或 1990 年以前的作品、研讨会论文、非拉丁文字的标题）不报此规则，而报 REF090。

**什么时候可能误报。** 真实的作品也可能不在任何索引里：内部报告、学位论文、地区性期刊、以译名引用的作品。标题错得太多也会检索不到。

**怎么处理。** 确认该作品确实存在、标题与发表时一致。如果作品存在、只是没有被索引，可以注明原因后消除：`% preflight: ignore[REF003] reason="内部报告"`。

核对之后，如需对某个条目消除此规则，在条目上方加注释：

    % preflight: ignore[REF003] reason="..."

对整个项目关闭此规则：在项目设置中写 `ignore-rules = ["REF003"]`。
